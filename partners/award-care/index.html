1 · Content engine

Real people, every week

  • One brand voice and a story library for each community, approved by Award Care.
  • 4–5 posts a week on Facebook and Instagram, and two on LinkedIn for referral partners and nurses.
  • Short vertical video, photo sets, family tips and celebrations.
  • Your approver signs off on each weekly batch before anything posts.
2 · Admissions engine

Families and referral partners

  • Every inquiry answered in minutes: missed calls get an instant text back; web forms alert that community's admissions team.
  • A "Choosing care" guide starts a 2–3 week follow-up for families who aren't ready yet.
  • Thank-you and next steps after each tour; review requests from happy families.
  • Case managers, discharge planners and physicians get a monthly update and a one-step "Refer a patient" form.
3 · Recruitment engine

Nurses, CNAs and therapists

  • A careers page for each community with real staff video and a text-to-apply number.
  • Every applicant gets an instant reply, a few screening questions and an interview slot.
  • Reminders cut no-shows; applicants who stall get a nudge.
  • Content for staff referral bonuses and CNA school partnerships.
4 · Ads, later

Only once the system is proven

  • From month 4, Meta and Google ads feed the machine: rehab and care searches in each market, and job ads for CNAs, LPNs and RNs.
  • We start where beds or shifts are emptiest, and only after tracking shows the cost per tour and per hire.

Monthly Community Content Day

One day on site a month keeps it real

What we capture
  • The administrator's "new standard" story: what's changed and what families can expect.
  • The Director of Nursing and therapy team: rehab wins and a day in the life.
  • 3–5 staff spotlights ("why I work here"), the strongest recruiting content there is.
  • Residents and families, only with signed consent.
  • Fresh staff headshots for the website, LinkedIn and name badges.
How it rotates

The three Sarasota communities fit in one day, so one day a month covers the group in five stops: Perry, Sarasota, Winter Garden, Jacksonville and Fort Pierce. Two days a month cuts the cycle roughly in half.

Between visits, short remote interviews and a simple phone-video upload link keep every community stocked.

Privacy and HIPAA

Nothing about a resident goes out without written permission

  • Consent first. Every resident or family featured signs a media authorization before any photo, video or story is used. Staff sign a release. We bring the forms and keep the records.
  • No health details in marketing. Inquiry and referral forms ask only what's needed to call back. Clinical details stay in your systems.
  • Compliant setup. The follow-up system runs in HIPAA mode, with a business associate agreement between Signature and Award Care.
  • You approve everything. Award Care signs off on every batch before it posts, under your compliance team's rules.
  • Works with your current partners. We plug in alongside your website team rather than replacing what works.
